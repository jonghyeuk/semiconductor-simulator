운전 절차

Standard operating procedure — English + 한국어
  1. Load the wafer into the chamber. (웨이퍼를 챔버에 로드한다)
  2. Evacuate — pump down to base pressure. (기준 압력까지 배기한다)
  3. Start gas and stabilize the flow. (가스를 투입해 유량을 안정화한다)
  4. Ignite plasma — check reflected power is low. (플라즈마를 점화하고 반사파가 낮은지 확인한다)
  5. Run the process. (공정을 진행한다)
  6. Stop the process. (공정을 정지한다)
  7. Vent the chamber. (챔버를 대기압으로 벤트한다)

영문 보고서 형식

Problem
Pump down failed to reach base pressure.
Observed log
VAC-101 interlock — pressure above limit.
Possible cause
Vacuum valve not fully open, or a leak.
Corrective action
Checked the valve and re-sealed the flange.
Result
Base pressure reached; process resumed normally.
